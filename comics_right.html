Signal And Silence (read this comic)

Status: Complete

Date: 2015 - 2019

Length: 100+ pages

 Adapted Randy Wyatt play. SIGNAL AND SILENCE is about three college friends --Jimmy, Nica and Ivan "Dorch" Dorchester-- all of whom are haunted by their shared history at an evangelical college. These days, Jimmy and Dorch run a comic store and run role-playing game sessions out of the basement with their newer friends Spiro and Jeremy. But over the span of several days, vital secrets are spilled that threaten to tear their bonds apart forever.
And then there's that gnome chieftain who shows up when Dorch is all alone...

Original play by Randy Wyatt.

Art & comics adaptation by Christopher López.
