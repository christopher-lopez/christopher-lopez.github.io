Well, it all started (for me, at least) in 1975. I grew up like every other kid, I guess. I was always a bit more quiet than the other kids on my block, and I loved staying indoors, reading comic books, drawing or playing with my Star Wars toys or my Adam computer. Growing up wasn't bad, except for my parents' divorce. High School was ok, too. I was pretty much a geek, but I had my set of friends, and it was allright. In my youthful naivete I thought we were going to hang out forever. I guess I'll always have some growing up to do. We still see each other these days, and it's not the same, but it's not bad.

Today, I'm very different from who I was back then, but not much has changed (go figure that one out). I'm a Computer Science graduate from FIU, but I have stopped collecting comic books. I was reading The Sandman for a while, but the series ended (so it goes). That book, in my opinion, is the finest example of literature I can think of right now. I still have my art, and I am doing more with it every day (adding a sculpture page!). I'm a video game freak, and I love my Sega Saturn (even though it's just about dead). I am a big fan of fighting games and RPGs. Musically, you can find me with Tori Amos or Ani DiFranco in the CD player just about as often as you'd find me with a Smashing Pumpkins or Beastie Boys album. I don't watch much TV, but I catch every episode of The Simpsons that I can. When I'm not home, I love going to the movies (Terry Gilliam and Kevin Smith are the best filmmakers out there).
