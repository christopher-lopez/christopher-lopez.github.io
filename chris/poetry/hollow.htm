"Hollow Man (with apologies to Mr. Eliot)"


I am thrust into the harsh light
by my own traitorous flesh.
Exposed, no soft tones caress my
body, only the burning heat.  
And I feel my skin begin to cry.
But not my eyes, I keep my tears on the inside.

I walk on, feeling the jagged 
rocks, scraping and slicing my soles,
but no matter where I go,
dark eyes follow, looming just out of reach,
safe in the inky veil of night.

My ankle twists and snaps,
& the floor rushes up to meet me.
Pain flows in as my blood ebbs out, slowly pooling all around.
Looking up, my eyes sting and squint, bathed in searing incandescence.
I taste the gritty sand and the metallic salt of blood and cold failure.
I spit.

Swirling shapes surround me
Peering, prying.  I try to hide 
my soul, holding my chest with both my bloody 
hands, but it is useless.  I am transparent.
