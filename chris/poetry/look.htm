Look!
                         AWay out.
                    Space & time ethereal
                  My eyes disembodied, see
               My fingers almost but not quite
             Connected to their respective hands
             Reaching for the calming brightness
             Yet, upon their arrival the charade
              is ended, the illusion of escape
                 denied and what was once
                    warm and light now
                        poisons and
                           rots.
