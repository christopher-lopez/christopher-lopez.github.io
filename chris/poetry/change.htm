And as I walked home, black mood unchanged
I came upon a woman the color of darkest night.
She sat alone on the walk, I know not why.
I thought nothing more of it and walked on by,
only hoping that she didn't ask for change.

So I continued with my strides, but I heard a mutter
My journey interrupted, I paused and wondered
What had she uttered?  So, I asked, arming
myself with refusals and denials.

With a single syllable, she shattered my armor
My defenses lay strewn across the floor
"Smile," she repeated, and a laugh broke across my face
I nodded and continued on my way, but
One step later I stopped, now twice prevented
from realizing my original destiny

Upon my heel I turned, my eyes searching
for the one who brought them joy.
The woman had now all but gone,
halfway down the sidewalk, I delivered
my thanks.  In turn, her nod sent 
me on way, her speech delivered,
the woman of purest ebony embarked
upon her own way, alone, and I now know why.
